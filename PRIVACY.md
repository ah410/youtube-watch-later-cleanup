# Privacy Policy

Watch Later Cleanup does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any user data.

The extension only runs when you click its toolbar icon and press Start, and only on the
active browser tab at that moment (`activeTab` permission). It injects a script
(`scripting` permission) that interacts directly with the YouTube page already open in
your browser, using your own logged-in session, to remove videos from your Watch Later
playlist the same way clicking "Remove from Watch Later" would.

No `host_permissions` are requested, no background scripts run on pages you haven't
clicked the extension on, and no servers other than YouTube's own are contacted. Nothing
about your browsing, your playlist, or your account is read, logged, or sent anywhere.

The extension is fully open source. You can review the entire source code at
https://github.com/ah410/youtube-watch-later-cleanup.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be committed to this file and reflected in
